# Brand Story

## Why "Zero"
Everything I'm building starts from the name. zer0Gravity isn't just what I called the business, it's the whole philosophy. Zero is where everything begins: the Big Bang came from nothing, binary starts at 0 before it becomes 1, and I started this business with nothing but an engineering degree, a drone, and an idea. "Zero" is my way of saying that starting point isn't a weakness, it's potential that hasn't been turned into something yet. That's the whole brand in one word.

## My Strapline
**"Elevate your vision, from zero to one."**
I moved on from my earlier line, "Elevate Your Vision", because it was fine, but it didn't say anything about me. "Zero to one" does. It's not just a catchy phrase, it's literally the mechanism of the brand: I take something (a property, an event, a moment, a vision) and elevate it into something more, the same way I'm trying to elevate myself from where I started to where I'm going.

## Zero and Zero Digital Twin
I think of myself as having two sides, and I built the brand around both of them instead of picking one.
- **Zero** is me right now: grounded, doing the work, present in the day-to-day grind of building a business.
- **Zero Digital Twin** is my imagination, my vision, my fantasy, and to me those are really the same thing wearing different names. It's the version of me that lives in the mythology I'm building alongside the business.

I'm a character caught between reality and fantasy, and I'm not trying to resolve that or pick a side. That tension is the brand. Zero is proof I can do the work in the real world; Zero Digital Twin is proof I can still dream past what the real world currently looks like. Neither one is more real than the other. The whole point of the brand is showing people that the gap between where you are and what you imagine isn't as far as it looks, because I'm living proof I'm trying to close it myself.

## How It Looks
My logo is a monochrome, single-eyed sentinel, a robotic bust with geometric shoulder pods. It's not decorative, it's meant to feel like a guardian watching over the vision.

My colours are locked in and I use them everywhere, the website, my video grading, my product design, the mythology I'm building:
- **Gold** is Zero: grounded, present, what I've achieved so far.
- **Wine** (deep burgundy/purple) is Zero Digital Twin: the fantasy, the vision, what's still ahead.
- **Black** holds both of them together.

Even my clothing reference comes from somewhere real: a gold/black/white patterned shirt passed down from my father, which I used as the actual design reference for Zero's digital-twin outfit in Blender. That's not a random aesthetic choice, it's my own history woven into the brand.

My original Instagram concept blended Afrofuturism, drone technology, and that same idea, a character caught between reality and fantasy, imagination and vision folded into one thing. That's still where I want this to live, poetic and visual, not a generic drone-service page with stock aerial shots and a price list.

## What I'm Actually Selling
I don't outsource the technology I fly. I design, build, and operate it myself, so clients get cinematic results today from someone actively building toward the next generation of aerial systems, not renting equipment out of someone else's supply chain. And that's really just the surface of it. I'm not selling a service, I'm offering a philosophy with a service attached: I use my technology to spark people's own potential, to turn someone's zero into a one. That's what separates zer0Gravity from every other drone operator or drone company. The engineering proves I can deliver. The philosophy is what makes it mine.

## Where I'm Taking This
Drone cinematography is my entry point, not my ceiling. I'm building toward storytelling and animation through the mythology I'm creating, deeper R&D and engineering, custom drone builds, AI-powered drones, and eventually a personal aerial "companion" device. I think about it the same way personal computers went from something shared and institutional to something every single person owned, I want drones to make that exact same shift, and I want to be the one who helps make it happen.

I lead this as a founder, not a faceless service business. The comparison I hold myself to is Apple's "tech guru" positioning, someone whose name and vision are inseparable from the product. Drone cinematography is just where that story starts.
