## What this means for zeroGravity

1. **The mid-tier rung is not empty.** NLYVisuals is in Orpington, has traded since 2019, and pitches almost exactly what you pitch (property, events, cinematic editing). They are your most direct competitor. Their owner's LinkedIn lists Bulgaria and they run a Bulgarian site too, so how active they are locally is worth checking.
2. **Nobody local sells FPV.** None of the local operators mention FPV. In London it's an add-on (+£399 FilmFolk, £450 FlyXpress) or premium (£1,500 AerialJohnny). An FPV property fly-through is your clearest local differentiator.
3. **Property stills are a price fight.** £140 (Man with a Drone) to £180 is the floor locally. Your £150 sits right in it, so you can't win on price, only on the FPV and editing.
4. **Events: you're priced below the market.** London mid-tier mini packages start at £120-250 but standard event coverage is £300-600, and UK wedding drone sessions run £400-900. Your £200 event standard is cheap. Fine for portfolio building, but note it for your raise triggers.
5. **Inspections are owned by roofers and surveyors** at £150-200. It's a commodity, with no editing needed. Worth it as quick cash and as a lead-in to Shadow's data collection, not as a brand play.
