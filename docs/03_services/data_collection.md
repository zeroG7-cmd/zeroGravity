# Drone Data Collection Services

## Status: Year 2 target (per Section 1.2 business aims), not sold in Year 1
This is a real, planned service for me, not a vague aspiration, I've already named it explicitly in my business aims (Section 1.2, mid-term: "Introduce data solutions... Launch mapping services") and priced it in Section 3.4 (£300+, Year 2 start).

## What this actually is
Surveying, inspection, and mapping using drone-mounted sensors, distinct from my photography/video work:
- **Surveying & mapping** — orthomosaic maps and site surveys for construction, land, or agriculture
- **Inspection** — aerial inspection of roofs, structures, or hard-to-reach sites for insurance, maintenance, or safety assessment
- **Data capture for R&D** — feeds into the LiDAR/mapping work I'm already doing in `zeroGravity-rnd` (RPLIDAR, computer vision, sensor fusion)

## Why Year 2, not now
This isn't a "someday" service I'm hand-waving at, but it isn't sellable yet either, because:
- I need a defined deliverable format (orthomosaic map? point cloud? inspection report?) before I can quote it
- It likely needs capability beyond A2 CofC (GVC or equivalent, depending on where and how I fly it)
- Equipment requirements (mapping-grade payload, RTK/GPS accuracy) go beyond the cinematography kit I use for real estate/events work

## What I need to do before I launch this
1. Define one specific first use case (e.g. small-site construction progress mapping) rather than trying to offer all three at once
2. Confirm the CAA authorisation level I actually need for that use case
3. Price a pilot project, possibly free or heavily discounted, with one local contact (construction, land, or agriculture) to get a real reference case before I set standard pricing
4. Only then set my Year 2 pricing properly, the current £300+ figure in Section 3.4 is a placeholder for me, not a researched number

## UK market reference (for later, not for quoting now)
Small-site drone mapping/surveying in the UK commonly runs from a few hundred pounds for small residential/commercial plots up to £1,000+ for larger commercial sites, but this varies heavily by deliverable complexity and I shouldn't treat it as a firm anchor until I've defined my own deliverable.
