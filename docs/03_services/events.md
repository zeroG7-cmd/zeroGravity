# Event Drone Coverage & Social Media Content

## Where this sits, honestly
This is where my own survey says the real demand is: events (46.9%) and social media content (43.8%) were my two most-selected use cases, far ahead of real estate (9.4%). Combined with 90.6% of respondents never having used a drone service before (a first-adopter market, not one I need to poach from a competitor) and 0% outright rejecting the idea, this is my strongest evidence for where to put my marketing effort first in Year 1.

## Packages

### Standard Event Coverage — £200
- Up to 2 hours on-site
- Aerial photos + short highlight video (60–90 seconds)
- Edited and colour graded
- Delivered within 3 working days

### Extended Event Coverage — £350
- Up to 4 hours on-site
- Full aerial photo set + extended highlight video (2–3 minutes)
- Social media cut-downs (2x 15-second clips for Instagram/TikTok)
- Delivered within 3 working days

### Social Media Content Package — £150
Given social media content is nearly as in-demand as events (43.8% vs 46.9%), I'm making this its own bookable package rather than just an add-on:
- Standalone short-form content shoot (no full event required)
- 3–4 vertical clips (15–30 seconds each), edited for Reels/TikTok/Shorts
- Suited to businesses wanting ongoing content rather than one-off event coverage

## What's included
- Pre-event site and airspace check
- Public liability insurance
- Weather contingency reschedule
- One round of revisions

## Why I'm confident in this pricing
65.6% of my survey respondents would pay £100 or more (37.5% in the £100–200 band, 28.1% in £200–500), and Quality was picked over Price by respondents (75.0% vs 46.9%) as the deciding purchase factor. That tells me I don't need to discount to compete, my audience isn't primarily price-driven.

## Booking notes
- Events require flight plan confirmation at least 3 days in advance (airspace/venue permissions)
- Outdoor events only at this stage; indoor venue flying is a future capability, not something I offer yet
- Deposit: 25% to confirm booking, balance due on delivery
